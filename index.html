Everyone at your institution signs in through your institution's own landing page at a web address ending in your institution code — for example your-lms.gov.ph/TVI-MANILA. There is no shared, generic login.

How login works

  • The public home page lists all institutions with a search box. Click your institution (or search by name/code) to open its landing page.
  • Each landing page has two tabs: Student and Staff / Teacher. Pick the one that matches your account.
  • The Student tab only accepts student accounts of that institution; the Staff/Teacher tab accepts staff, teacher and content-builder accounts of that institution.
  • Platform administrators use the small Admin button on the public home page (a separate secure modal).
No self-signup. Accounts are created for you by your institution's Staff. If you can't log in, contact your Staff administrator to confirm your account is active and that you're using the correct tab.
Secure session

Sessions last 24 hours and are protected by a secure, browser-only token — no password is stored in the page.

After login

You land on your role's home screen automatically. The left menu shows only what your role can use.

Any device

The platform works on desktop, tablet and phone. On mobile, tap the menu icon to open navigation.

Subscription & AI Credits

Your institution runs on a subscription plan that sets two kinds of limits: capacity (how many students and enrollments you can have) and AI credits (how much AI generation and grading you can do). Staff can see current usage on the Staff Console.

Capacity limits

  • Students — the max number of student accounts. Creating a student uses one slot.
  • Enrollments — the max number of course enrollments. Each enrollment (by staff or student self-enroll) uses one.
  • Plans are stackable — the oldest active plan is used first, then the next.

The four AI credit pools

  • CS / CBC — Competency Standard & Curriculum generation (1 credit = 1 generation).
  • Course Content — module lists, lesson outlines & content, exam & essay items, thumbnails.
  • Slides — AI slide design + slide background images.
  • Essay Grading — 1 credit = 1 essay/activity/code answer auto-graded.

When credits run low

AI generation pauses when a pool is exhausted. Your platform administrator can add more credits to your plan.

Grading is safe

If grading credits run out, essays simply wait for a teacher to grade them manually — students are never blocked.

Daily vs period

Content & slide pools have a daily cap and a total (period) budget. Grading has a total budget only — no daily cap.

Authentication & Scopes

Every request includes your API key in the X-API-Key header. Each key carries a set of scopes that decide what it can do — issued when Staff generate the key.

Example request

curl -X GET https://your-lms.gov.ph/api/v1/external/ping \
  -H "X-API-Key: tldsk_your_api_key_here"
Key format: keys start with tldsk_ followed by 48 hex characters (54 chars total). The full key is shown only once at creation — store it safely.

PLATFORM

Security & Privacy

Your institution's data is isolated and protected. Here's what keeps it safe.

Institution isolation

Staff and teachers only ever see their own institution's accounts, courses and data.

Strong passwords

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt (never stored in plain text). Deactivated accounts can't log in.

Secure sessions

Login uses a secure, browser-only session token that expires after 24 hours. Login attempts are rate-limited.

Safe content

Lesson content is sanitized before it's saved, preventing malicious code from being injected.

Audit & API logs

Sensitive changes and every external API request are logged for accountability.

Scoped API keys

Integration keys are hashed, scoped to specific actions, and limited to your institution's data.

HELP

Frequently Asked Questions

I can't log in — what should I check?

Make sure you're on your institution's landing page (URL ends with your code), on the correct tab (Student vs Staff/Teacher), and that your account is active. If unsure, ask your institution's Staff to confirm your account.

Who can create accounts?

Staff create all accounts for the institution (Student, Teacher, Staff, and Content Builder). Students cannot self-register.

What's the difference between a course and a course instance?

A course is the master content (modules, lessons, exams). A course instance is a specific section/batch of that course with its own dates and cap. Students enroll into instances; content is shared, never duplicated.

What are AI credits and what happens when they run out?

Credits meter AI usage across four pools (CS/CBC, Course Content, Slides, Grading). When a pool is empty, that AI action pauses until your plan is topped up. Essay grading is special: when its credits run out, essays simply wait for a teacher to grade them manually — students are never blocked.

Is there an "uploader" account?

The account that builds and uploads content is called Developer (Content Builder) in the system. There is no separate "uploader" role — the Content Builder is that role.

How does a student earn a certificate?

Complete every module and pass every assessment in the course. The course then auto-completes and a verifiable PDF certificate (with QR code) becomes available to download.

Can I trust AI-graded essays?

AI grades against your rubric and gives feedback, but a teacher can review and override any AI score. The teacher's grade always wins.
