THE CHALLENGE

Every institution wants to go digital.
Almost none can afford to build the content.

A learning platform is the easy part. The real cost — the part that stalls most TVIs — is producing the actual courses, lesson content, exams, presentations, and the TESDA Competency Standards and Curriculum documents. Doing that by hand takes months of specialist time.

Content takes months

Writing modules, lessons, exam banks and slide decks for even a handful of qualifications is a huge, slow job.

Developers are expensive

Hiring instructional designers to build and keep content current is a recurring cost most institutions can't sustain.

TESDA paperwork is manual

Competency Standards and Competency-Based Curricula are formatted by hand — error-prone and time-consuming.

Grading eats teacher time

Marking essays and activities for hundreds of students, every term, is hours no teacher has to spare.

What if the platform built all of that for you?

AI Genesis uses AI to generate courses, exams, slides and TESDA competency documents in minutes — and grades student work automatically. You launch this term, not next year.

THE PLATFORM

One platform. The whole training lifecycle.

AI course & content generation

Generate module lists, full lesson content, exam question banks and essay items from a topic — reviewed and edited by your team before publishing.

AI slide decks & visuals

Turn any module into a designed presentation with AI-generated slides and background images, exported to PDF.

TESDA CS & CBC generators

Produce formatted Competency Standards and Competency-Based Curriculum documents (.docx) — basic, common and core units, all AI-assisted.

AI auto-grading

Multiple-choice scores instantly; essays, activities and code are graded by AI against a rubric — with teacher review and override.

Assessments & progress

Timed exams with configurable attempts and passing scores, plus per-student module and assessment tracking with at-risk detection.

Verifiable certificates

Auto-issued on completion with a QR code anyone can scan to verify authenticity — no login required.

Multi-institution & sections

Each institution's data is isolated. Run courses as sections/batches with their own dates, caps and teacher assignments.

SIS integration (API)

A scoped, secure external API lets your Student Information System sync accounts, enrollments, grades and certificates.

Cloud-hosted & secure. Runs on enterprise cloud infrastructure with encrypted passwords, isolated institution data, audit logging, and auto-scaling servers sized to your student count.
